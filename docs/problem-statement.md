# Problem Statement — E1: Student Dropout Predictor

## The Specific Audience Affected

**Primary:** Academic coordinators, faculty advisors, and Student Welfare Officers at Indian universities — specifically CHARUSAT (Charotar University of Science & Technology), which has 5,000+ students across 6 constituent institutes and 20+ programs.

**Secondary:** Students at risk of dropout who currently have no systematic support mechanism until they are already in crisis.

---

## Why Existing Solutions Don't Solve It

| Existing Approach | Gap |
|-------------------|-----|
| Manual coordinator checks | Reactive, not proactive — intervention happens after failure, not before |
| Attendance SMS alerts | Single-metric — doesn't factor in marks, financial stress, social issues, backlogs |
| University ERP dashboards | Show raw data only — no risk scoring, no prioritisation, no intervention plans |
| End-of-semester reviews | Too late — by Semester 4, dropout decisions are already made |
| General counselling | Demand-driven — only students who seek help get it; most at-risk students don't |

**No existing tool at CHARUSAT (or most Indian universities) combines multiple academic + behavioural + financial signals into a single, AI-generated, actionable risk profile.**

---

## Quantified Pain

- **India loses 40% of engineering students** before graduation (AICTE 2023 data)
- **CHARUSAT estimate:** ~150–200 students per year are at significant dropout risk
- **Cost per dropout to university:** ₹1.5–3L in lost fees + administrative overhead
- **Cost per dropout to student:** Career disruption, lost opportunity, family financial strain
- **Coordinator overload:** 1 faculty advisor per 60–80 students on average — impossible to monitor manually
- **Detection lag:** On average, dropout risk is identified 2–3 semesters after warning signs first appear
- **Intervention window:** The critical window is **Semester 1–2** for Year 1 students and **Semester 3–4** for Year 2–3 students — currently missed entirely

---

## Why This Problem Matters Now

1. **NEP 2020** mandates outcome-based education — universities are now measured on graduation rates and employability. High dropout = institutional penalty.
2. **Rising financial stress post-COVID** — 34% more Indian students report financial difficulty in 2024 vs 2019 (NSSO data)
3. **Mental health crisis** — 1 in 5 college students in India experiences clinical-level anxiety or depression (NIMHANS 2024)
4. **AI is ready** — IBM Bob and watsonx.ai make it possible to analyse multi-factor student data in real time and generate actionable insights, something that was not feasible 2 years ago

---

## The Problem in One Sentence

Academic coordinators have all the data they need to prevent student dropout, but no AI-powered system to connect the dots, prioritise at-risk students, and generate actionable intervention plans before it's too late.
